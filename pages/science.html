Target selection

Improving how targets are selected for drug discovery

We work together with scientists across our partner organisations to identify areas where our research programme will enhance target identification and prioritisation either by integrating and analysing data, or by generating new data in our therapeutic areas of interest.

Drugs against genetically validated targets are more likely to progress through clinical trials. The influx of new data from modern genetics and genomics allows targets to be assessed in a comprehensive genome-wide context. In addition, feasibility of new technologies in gene editing, single cell sequencing and cell models allow new types of data to be generated to inform target selection.

We bring these approaches together in an integrated pre-competitive research programme.

Target Identification

Define targets with significant associations to disease biology, using whole-genome approaches that assess candidates in a comprehensive genome-wide context.

Examines:

  • Genetic and genomic association evidence
  • Functional genomics and perturbation screens
  • Disease-relevant cellular systems

Target Prioritisation

Refine the list on the strength of association evidence together with additional target and disease parameters, so effort goes where it is most likely to translate.

Examines:

  • Strength and consistency of evidence
  • Tractability by drug modality
  • Likely target-based safety risks
Research Programme

Linking targets and diseases

Our research programme generates and analyses data to connect targets to diseases, assess the strength of that evidence, and prioritise targets for drug discovery. This includes evidence that causally links targets and diseases, as well as foundational data that deepens our understanding of biological processes and disease progression.

We combine whole-genome approaches with high-throughput methods in the most relevant cellular systems, drawing on the expertise of all our partners in emerging and established technologies across three therapy areas.

Informatics

Core informatics and data generation pipelines

Our core bioinformatics work brings together the data relevant to targets so that drug discovery scientists can explore it simply and seamlessly. We cover many data types relevant to human disease biology and target identification, using expertise from Open Targets partners and beyond.

Flagship resource

Open Targets Platform

Our approach to target identification and prioritisation is brought to life in the Platform, which integrates public data relevant to the association between targets and diseases and provides additional data and tools for prioritisation. It is an open source project, and continues to evolve as we bring new features and data to bear.
