Our research

Science built around what works in the clinic

About the research programme

Drugs against genetically validated targets are more than twice as likely to succeed in clinical trials. Our research programme generates the large-scale experimental data needed to identify those targets and put them in front of drug discovery teams.

Oncology

Large-scale CRISPR screens and somatic genomics to find and validate cancer targets, with a focus on resistance mechanisms and tumour dependencies.

Neurodegeneration

Genetic dissection of diseases like Alzheimer's and Parkinson's, combining GWAS, multi-omics, and human iPSC models to move from signal to mechanism.

Immunology & inflammation

Decoding the genetics of immune-mediated diseases to surface targets with the clearest causal evidence across conditions like IBD and RA.

How we work

Our principles

A systematic approach

We leverage publicly available data to systematically identify and prioritise the best targets to safely and effectively treat rare and common diseases.

Open exchange of ideas

Science advances faster when ideas are shared openly. All our data, tools, and publications are made publicly available as soon as possible.

Multidisciplinary collaboration

We believe in non-exclusive partnerships that foster the free exchange of ideas, knowledge, and data.
